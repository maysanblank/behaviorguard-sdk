HOW TO READ
  Row 1 is the control: if it is far from the numbers of `python tools/reproduce_db.py`,
  the re-extraction is wrong, not the representation - check that first.
  Row 2 vs 1  = idle damage in the real world, at a valid operating point.
  Row 3 vs 2  = WHETHER C-23 (shipped, on by default) really helps.
               This is the most important question in this table.
  Row 3 vs 1  = how close C-23 brings things back to before idle existed.
  Row 4 vs 1  = the cost of C-23 when there is NO idle at all (should be ~zero).
  Row 5 vs 1  = the pure cost of C-24 canonicalisation (OFF by default).

  Look at EER and AUC first: both are independent of the operating point. FRR/FAR
  depend on the q tuned per condition, so they can trade against each other
  without anything really improving.
